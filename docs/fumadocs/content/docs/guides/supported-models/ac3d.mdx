## Architecture

Per the paper (arXiv 2411.18673), AC3D locates camera-motion information in low-frequency early-timestep components and conditions only a subset of layers, improving control precision and visual quality. Both 2B and 5B ControlNet checkpoints are recorded; the adapter preserves official 2B ControlNet shape settings.

## Usage notes

AC3D stays in its dedicated environment because the official torch 2.4/CUDA 12.4 stack is not ABI-compatible with the unified environment. Inputs accept either a RealEstate10K-style root or a direct source video plus 19-column camera pose rows.
