ATI (Any Trajectory Instruction) turns trajectory drawing into video control: draw point trajectories over an input image and the Wan2.1-14B-based model animates objects, camera, or both along them.

## What it is for

`ati-wan21-14b` generates camera-controlled video: a camera trajectory in, a clip that follows it out.

Per the upstream release, ATI injects dense point-track conditioning into the Wan2.1 I2V backbone through a trajectory instruction module. At load time WorldFoundry combines the ATI weights (bytedance-research/ATI) with locally staged Wan2.1 I2V 480P base components. It is published by ByteDance.

Tasks: camera-controlled video and image-to-video. Modalities: image in, video out.

Suggested benchmarks: [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench) (Evaluates trajectory/camera-motion adherence).

## Runtime contract

Inputs `prompt` and `input-path` (bundled samples are used when omitted); artifacts `ati-wan21-14b.mp4`.

CLI task profiles: `default`. WorldFoundry binds `ATIPipeline`.

Family status: **Runner parity pending**.

Inputs are a prompt, an image, and dense point tracks. Packed tracks have shape [N,121,1,3], with x/y pixel coordinates multiplied by 8; visibility may be normalized [0,1] or upstream quantized [0,8], consistently within each track. Validated with local ATI and Wan2.1 I2V 480P checkpoints: the pipeline generates an 81-frame video on GPU.

Point-trajectory adherence and parity with official outputs are not yet measured.

Checkpoint loading combines the ATI weights with locally staged Wan2.1 I2V 480P components. The 14B backbone needs a high-memory GPU.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Generate a camera-controlled clip with `wf run ati-wan21-14b --pipeline.task-profile default`.

It runs in the unified environment `worldfoundry-unified-cu128` (Python 3.11, CUDA 12.8).

Weights: `bytedance-research/ATI` and `Wan-AI/Wan2.1-I2V-14B-480P`. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh ati-wan21-14b`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
