In this catalog the official infer-only runtime is vendored in-tree, with the chunk-wise checkpoint staged as the local default and frame-wise/++ variants selectable by config override.

## Architecture

Per the paper (arXiv 2602.02214), a Wan2.1-based teacher is distilled into causal students with chunk-wise or frame-wise attention; the ++ variants push to 1-2 step sampling. Recorded checkpoints include chunk-wise plus frame-wise and two ++ few-step variants under zhuhz22/Causal-Forcing.

## Usage notes

The adapter executes the vendored official inference.py path; full parity requires local Wan2.1 weights plus the selected Causal-Forcing checkpoint/config pair. Supply benchmark prompt lists separately through your local input configuration.
