Causal-rCM applies NVIDIA's rCM consistency distillation to causal video generation: it streams video autoregressively over latent-frame chunks with distilled few-step sampling, making it a low-latency interactive world-model candidate. WorldFoundry runs the official Apache-2.0 causal inference stack.

## What it is for

Use `causal-rcm` to step a world forward with actions and render the resulting frames.

Per the paper (arXiv 2510.08431), rCM combines score-regularized consistency distillation for few-step sampling. The vendored stack drops the upstream imaginaire framework — WorldFoundry's own lazy_config, easy_io, and logging back it — and the block-causal mask predicates were lifted into worldfoundry/core/attention/sparse/block_pattern.py, verified against upstream across 702 mask and 162 chunk-schedule configurations.

Tasks: autoregressive video generation, streaming video generation, interactive world model, and text-to-video. Aliases: `rcm`, `causal_rcm`, `rcm-causal`, and `causal-rcm-wan2.1`. Modalities: text and image in, video out.

Suggested benchmarks: [VBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/vbench) (Video quality evaluation for streaming text/image-to-video).

## Runtime contract

Inputs `prompt` and `input-path` (bundled samples are used when omitted); options `frames`, `steps`, `seed`, `plan-only`, `timeout-seconds`, `model-size`, and 6 more; artifacts `generated_world`, `generated_video`, and `causal-rcm.mp4`.

CLI task profiles: `default`. WorldFoundry binds `CausalRCMPipeline`.

Family status: **Runner parity pending**.

first_chunk_t and chunk_t select the schedule: c1-1 frame-wise gives the lowest streaming latency, c3-3 more throughput. Distilled 4-step sampling is the default; distilled=False samples the multi-step teacher with CFG. context_from_last_step=True saves one clean forward per chunk but requires a checkpoint trained for noisy context. The released entrypoint is prompt/image conditioned with no discrete action space.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Step the world with `wf run causal-rcm --pipeline.task-profile default`.

Weights: `worstcoder/rcm-Wan` and `worstcoder/Wan`. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh causal-rcm`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
