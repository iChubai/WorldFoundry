Cosmos Predict 2.5 is NVIDIA's **world foundation model** for physical AI: text, image, or video in, world video out. The paper is [Cosmos Predict 2.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00062) (see also [research.nvidia.com/labs/dir/cosmos-predict2.5](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dir/cosmos-predict2.5)). WorldFoundry assembles the model from its shared denoiser, conditioner, scheduler, decoder, and loader roles **without Diffusers**.

<ModelPaperFigures modelId="cosmos-predict-2.5" locale="en" />

Two sizes are available: **2B** and **14B**. NVIDIA access is **gated**; the license is `other:nvidia-open-model-license`.

The validated recipe is the **2B text-to-world** route at **1280×704, 93 frames, 35 steps, 16 fps**, flow-UniPC scheduling. The 14B route has not been validated end to end on GPU. Aliases `cosmos-predict2p5` and `cosmos-predict-2p5` resolve here.

## What it is for

Predict future world video from text, a first frame, or a video prefix. Variants are `cosmos-predict-2.5-2b` and `cosmos-predict-2.5-14b`. Default artifact: `cosmos-predict2.5-2b.mp4` (14B writes `cosmos-predict2.5-14b.mp4`). Score world-generation quality on [WorldModelBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/worldmodelbench) and physical plausibility on [Physics-IQ](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/physics-iq).

## Runtime contract

`CosmosPredict2p5Pipeline` unifies text/image/video-to-world in one **rectified-flow** model with a **Reason1** language conditioner and a shared Wan VAE. Prompt conditioning, positive guidance, image/video latent conditioning, and decoding all run through the shared native diffusion roles. Upstream NVIDIA cookbooks also describe robot / action-conditioned and policy variants (RoboCasa, LIBERO); WorldFoundry does not provide them.

## Running in WorldFoundry

The pipeline runs in `worldfoundry-unified-cu128`. Launch `wf run cosmos-predict-2.5-2b` with `--pipeline.task-profile text-to-world-validation`. Stage `nvidia/Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B` (and 14B if you are testing that line) under NVIDIA's access rules.

The network is an inference-only PyTorch `MinimalV1LVGDiT` that follows `nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-predict2.5` at `a2c298b0a3df3778b973fe65e9e58877b292d8a7` and loads the NVIDIA EMA weights.

## Minimal eval

Import [WorldModelBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/worldmodelbench) official-shaped results and read `world_model_average`. `official-run` needs `WORLDFOUNDRY_WORLDMODELBENCH_JUDGE`; full judge execution has not been validated.
