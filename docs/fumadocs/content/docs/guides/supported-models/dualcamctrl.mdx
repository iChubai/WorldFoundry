DualCamCtrl controls video generation with a camera trajectory plus a depth image: the RGB and depth conditions together pin down scene geometry so the generated camera move stays physically grounded. In this catalog the runner is verified and follows the official RGB/depth/camera demo contract.

## Architecture

The default demo contract follows the official recipe: an image, a depth image, a camera .torch trajectory file, 61 frames at 320x480, 50 steps. The in-tree inference runtime lives under synthesis/visual_generation/dualcamctrl; internals beyond the recorded contract are not stored here.

## Usage notes

Open-source users should rely on the default Hugging Face cache/download path; pre-staged local assets can be passed through checkpoint_path, base_model_path, or WORLDFOUNDRY_MODEL_DIR.
