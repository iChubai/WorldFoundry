## What it is for

`hunyuanworld-voyager` is a world generation model.

Per the upstream release, Voyager combines a HunyuanVideo-based generator with joint RGB-D output and a world-cache mechanism for long-range consistency. The official case1 defaults are num_frames=49, infer_steps=50, flow_shift=7.0, embedded_cfg_scale=6.0, i2v_stability=true. It is published by Tencent (Hunyuan).

Tasks: world, video, and world model. Output modalities: video.

Suggested benchmarks: [WorldScore](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/worldscore) (Scoring for camera-navigable generated worlds), [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench) (Camera-path adherence evaluation).

Set WORLDFOUNDRY_VOYAGER_SOURCE_ROOT and WORLDFOUNDRY_VOYAGER_PYTHON. The Tencent community license excludes use in the EU, UK, and South Korea and restricts redistribution.

The official case1 requests 49 frames but the latent-concat I2V pipeline discards four conditioning frames, yielding 45 video frames; longer voyages need iterative extension. Depth quality is generative, not sensor-grade — validate before metric use. Validation covers the official case1 example only; arbitrary camera paths, long-range extension, and quantitative depth or camera fidelity are not validated.
