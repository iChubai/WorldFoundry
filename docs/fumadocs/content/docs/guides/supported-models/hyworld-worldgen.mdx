HY-World 2.0 Trajectory Renderer is one stage of Tencent's world-generation chain, cataloged separately: it renders camera-controlled video from a pre-materialized HY-World scene point cloud (render_results/global_pcd.ply) along a camera JSON. It intentionally loads no model checkpoint — it is a renderer, not a generator.

## Architecture

The unified runtime includes an in-tree torch point-splat fallback and does not require PyTorch3D. Official source is vendored at a pinned revision (license: tencent-hunyuan-community); this route is the official trajectory-render stage and does not claim full five-stage world generation.

## Usage notes

Provide an image, camera JSON, and a scene_path pointing at a pre-materialized HY-World scene. Scene materialization belongs to the separate full HY-World generation pipeline.
