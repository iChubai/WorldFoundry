## What it is for

`kandinsky6-lite-pretrain` generates video from a text prompt, optionally conditioned on a still image.

Tasks: text-to-video, image-to-video, and audio video generation. Modalities: text and image in, video out.

## Runtime contract

Inputs `prompt` and `image`; artifacts `generated_video` and `kandinsky6-lite-pretrain.mp4`.

CLI task profiles: `text-to-video`, `image-to-video`, `audio-video-generation`, and `video_generation`. WorldFoundry binds `Kandinsky6Pipeline`.
