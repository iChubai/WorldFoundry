## What it is for

`krea-realtime-video` is a text-to-video model: a prompt in, a video clip out.

Upstream describes it as a 14B autoregressive/streaming video model distilled for realtime block-wise generation (weights at krea/krea-realtime-video, apache-2.0). Generation proceeds in blocks controlled by num_blocks, with a prompt and optional video as input.

Tasks: realtime video generation, text-to-video, video-to-video, and streaming video. Aliases: `krea-rt-14b`. Modalities: text and video in, video out.

Suggested benchmarks: [VBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/vbench) (Baseline quality check for streaming T2V output).
