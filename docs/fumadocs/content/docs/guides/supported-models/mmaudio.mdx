MMAudio generates soundtracks for silent video: given a video (and optionally a text prompt), it synthesizes audio that is semantically and temporally aligned with the visuals. Use it to add audio to clips produced by the video models; its artifact type is generated_audio, not video.

## What it is for

Use `mmaudio` for audio generation.

MMAudio is a multimodal joint-training flow-matching model that conditions audio generation on video frames and text (per its publication). It is published by Sony AI / University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

Tasks: text-to-audio, video-to-audio, and audio generation. Aliases: `hkchengrex/MMAudio`. Modalities: text and video in, audio out.

## Runtime contract

Inputs `prompt` and `input-path` (bundled samples are used when omitted); options `steps`, `duration`, and `variant`; artifacts `mmaudio.wav`.

CLI task profiles: `interactive-video`. WorldFoundry binds `MMAudioPipeline`.

Family status: **Runner parity pending**.

Typical workflow: generate video with any T2V/I2V model, then feed the clip to MMAudio for a soundtrack.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Generate audio with `wf run mmaudio --pipeline.task-profile interactive-video`.

It runs in the unified environment `worldfoundry-unified-cu128` (Python 3.11, CUDA 12.8).

Weights: `hkchengrex/MMAudio`. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh mmaudio`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
