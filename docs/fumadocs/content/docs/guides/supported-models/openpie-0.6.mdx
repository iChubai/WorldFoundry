## What it is for

Pick `openpie-0.6` when you want the catalog VLA policy: instruction and observation in, an action trace out. Use it to roll the recorded policy, not to train a new one.

The runtime contract is the FLA policy factory from the exla-ai/fla repository (pinned head 1ac45102). Architecture internals are not recorded in this repository.

Catalog tasks include vla, robot policy, and policy rollout. Catalog aliases include `pi-0.6` and `openpie0.6`.
