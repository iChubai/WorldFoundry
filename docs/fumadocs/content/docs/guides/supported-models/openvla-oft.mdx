OpenVLA-OFT applies optimized fine-tuning to OpenVLA — parallel decoding, continuous action heads, and chunking — and is fully GPU-validated in this catalog: an A100 restored all 7.54B parameters, produced deterministic finite 8x7 action chunks across multiple inputs at 14.47 GiB peak VRAM, and all five declared LIBERO checkpoints completed real inference (MIT license).

## Architecture

The OFT recipe replaces autoregressive token decoding with parallel decoding plus an L1 continuous action head and 8-step chunking over the 7B OpenVLA backbone, with proprio and noisy-action projectors in-tree. Attention is hardware-resolved: FlashAttention 2 on Ampere/Ada/Hopper, PyTorch SDPA fallback elsewhere including Blackwell.

## Usage notes

Pick the LIBERO suite checkpoint matching your split (spatial, object, goal, etc.); official LIBERO observation inputs are supported directly. Training, RLDS processing, TensorFlow tooling, and LIBERO benchmark loops are excluded.
