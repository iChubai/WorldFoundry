Self-Forcing bridges the train-test gap in autoregressive video diffusion: by simulating the inference process during training — conditioning each frame on its own previously generated frames — it enables real-time streaming text-to-video on a single GPU (arXiv 2506.08009). WorldFoundry runs the official inference-only runtime, which needs the Self-Forcing DMD checkpoint plus Wan2.1 weights.

## What it is for

`self-forcing` generates video from a text prompt, optionally conditioned on a still image.

It is published by Adobe Research / The University of Texas at Austin.

Tasks: text-to-video, image-to-video, and autoregressive video generation. Aliases: `Self-Forcing`, `selfforcing`, and `self_forcing`. Modalities: text and image in, video out.

Suggested benchmarks: [VBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/vbench) (Video quality for streaming text-to-video).
