SolarWM is an open data and scalable training foundation for camera-controlled, long-horizon video world models. It supports Wan2.2 5B/14B, LTX-2.5 22B, and MiniMax-H3 33B backbones; the 5B line releases the full bidirectional FM, TF-AnyFlow, and SGF progression.

## What it is for

Use `solarwm-wan2.2-5b` for catalog world-model inference. Choose it when you need action-conditioned future frames rather than a standalone clip generator.

The complete 5B route adapts Wan2.2 through Stage0.5 bidirectional flow matching, Stage1 teacher-forced AnyFlow, and Stage2 distribution matching via self-gradient forcing. The final causal model performs four-step autoregressive camera-conditioned rollout from short training clips. It is published by SolarWM authors.

Catalog tasks include camera-conditioned-world-model, long-horizon-video-generation, interactive-video-generation, and camera-conditioned-long-horizon-video. Catalog aliases include `solar-wm`, `solarwm-wan2.2-5b`, and `solarwm-wan22-5b`. Documented outputs include video. Also recorded: `solarwm-wan2.2-14b`, `solarwm-ltx-22b`, `solarwm-h3-33b`.

Recorded evaluation entry points include [WorldModelBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/worldmodelbench) (Long-horizon camera-controlled world consistency).
