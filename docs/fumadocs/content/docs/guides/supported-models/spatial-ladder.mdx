## What it is for

`spatial-ladder` answers questions about multimodal inputs.

A 3B-parameter vision-language model; the upstream release describes its progressive spatial-reasoning training design.

Tasks: multimodal reasoning, spatial reasoning, image question answering, and video question answering. Aliases: `spatial_ladder` and `hongxingli/SpatialLadder-3B`.
