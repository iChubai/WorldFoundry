## What it is for

`spatial-reasoner` answers questions about multimodal inputs.

A vision-language model with explicit 3D-aware spatial reasoning stages (per the upstream release at ccvl/SpatialReasoner). It takes a prompt together with an image or video. It is published by Johns Hopkins University (CCVL).

Tasks: multimodal reasoning, spatial reasoning, image question answering, and video question answering. Aliases: `spatial_reasoner` and `ccvl/SpatialReasoner`.
