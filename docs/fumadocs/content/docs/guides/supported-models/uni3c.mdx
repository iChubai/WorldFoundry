Uni3C from Alibaba DAMO Academy unifies camera control and human-motion control for image-to-video generation: the PCDController stage steers the camera while the unified stage combines camera plus human motion (RealisDance-DiT). WorldFoundry runs both official stage-two inference paths with public Apache-2.0 checkpoints (ewrfcas/Uni3C, theFoxofSky/RealisDance-DiT) on top of the Wan2.1-I2V-14B-720P base.

## What it is for

`uni3c` conditions video generation on camera poses.

Three checkpoints are used: ewrfcas/Uni3C as the PCDController, Wan-AI/Wan2.1-I2V-14B-720P-Diffusers as the camera base, and theFoxofSky/RealisDance-DiT as the unified camera-plus-human model. Pre-rendered control bundles are explicit runtime inputs.

Tasks: image-to-video, camera-controlled video, and human motion-controlled video. Aliases: `PCDController` and `alibaba-damo-academy/Uni3C`. Modalities: image in, video out.

Suggested benchmarks: [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench) (Camera-trajectory adherence under explicit control).

## Runtime contract

Inputs `prompt`, `input-path`, and `render-path` (bundled samples are used when omitted); options `mode`, `num-frames`, `fps`, `max-area`, `seed`, and `num-gpus`; artifacts `generated_video` and `uni3c.mp4`.

CLI task profiles: `image-camera-control-video`. WorldFoundry binds `Uni3CPipeline`.

Family status: **Verified**.

Defaults for `image-camera-control-video`: fps=16, seed=0.

Choose the camera-only or unified camera-plus-human route and provide the matching pre-rendered control bundle. Validated on an H100 with the released checkpoints: the default PCDController camera route runs end to end.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Generate a camera-controlled clip with `wf run uni3c --pipeline.task-profile image-camera-control-video`.

It runs in the dedicated environment `uni3c` (Python 3.10, CUDA 12.4, torch==2.6.0+cu124).

Weights: `ewrfcas/Uni3C`, `Wan-AI/Wan2.1-I2V-14B-720P-Diffusers`, and `theFoxofSky/RealisDance-DiT`. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh uni3c`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
