UniAnimate-DiT is Alibaba ali-vilab's human image animation model built on Wan2.1-I2V: it animates a person photo to follow a driving pose sequence. The in-tree route is wired with local checkpoint assets and exposes max_frames, resolution, step count, and seed controls.

## Architecture

Built on the Wan2.1 image-to-video diffusion transformer with pose-sequence conditioning for human animation (checkpoint: ZheWang123/UniAnimate-DiT). Deeper conditioning internals are not recorded in this repository.

## Usage notes

Runner parity is pending; per-run artifact checks live outside the catalog. Prepare a clean subject photo and a driving pose sequence for best results.
