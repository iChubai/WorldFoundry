VideoCrafter is Tencent AI Lab's open video generation toolbox; this family entry aggregates the in-tree infer-only runtimes for VideoCrafter1 I2V, VideoCrafter1 T2V, and VideoCrafter2 T2V. The per-variant entries (videocrafter1-i2v, videocrafter1-t2v, videocrafter2-t2v) hold the individual routes; checkpoint provenance is tracked here at the family level to avoid duplicate HF aliases.

## Architecture

U-Net latent video diffusion models: VideoCrafter1 offers T2V (up to 1024x576) and I2V; VideoCrafter2 improves the T2V model by decoupling appearance learning (low-quality video + high-quality image data). Training, dataset, and download helpers are intentionally excluded from the in-tree packages.

## Usage notes

Runner parity is pending; exhaustive upstream benchmark parity is recorded as a longer validation stage. License note from the manifest: the VideoCrafter2 HF card exposes Apache-2.0, but the older Text2Video/Image2Video cards need license review.
