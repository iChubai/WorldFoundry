VideoCrafter1 T2V is the text-to-video member of the VideoCrafter1 release, generating clips up to 1024x576 from a prompt. WorldFoundry runs the official text-to-video 1024 path for inference, with local checkpoint, fps, frame count, and seed controls.

## What it is for

`videocrafter1-t2v` generates a video clip from a text prompt.

U-Net latent video diffusion at up to 1024x576 (per the upstream release). It is published by Tencent AI Lab.

Tasks: text-to-video and video generation. Aliases: `videocrafter1_t2v`. Modalities: text in, video out.

Suggested benchmarks: [EvalCrafter](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/evalcrafter) (Companion benchmark of the VideoCrafter effort).

Not [VideoCrafter](/docs/guides/supported-models/videocrafter) and [VideoCrafter2 T2V](/docs/guides/supported-models/videocrafter2-t2v). Score on [EvalCrafter](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/evalcrafter).

## Runtime contract

Inputs `prompt` (bundled samples are used when omitted); options `frames`, `fps`, `height`, `width`, `steps`, `seed`, and 2 more; artifacts `generated_video` and `videocrafter1-t2v.mp4`.

CLI task profiles: `interactive-video`. WorldFoundry binds `VideoCrafter1T2VPipeline`.

Family status: **Runner parity pending**.

Runner parity is pending. Prefer VideoCrafter2 T2V for better visual quality from the same family, or the DiT entries for current-generation quality.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Launch `wf run videocrafter1-t2v --pipeline.task-profile interactive-video` to sample the clip. The command builder above switches `--pipeline.task-profile` among recorded tasks and still emits `wf run`.

It runs in the unified environment `worldfoundry-unified-cu128` (Python 3.11, CUDA 12.8).

Weights: `VideoCrafter/Text2Video-1024`. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh videocrafter1-t2v`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
