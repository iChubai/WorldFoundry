## Architecture

U-Net latent video diffusion at up to 1024x576 (per the upstream release). Checkpoint provenance is tracked on the family entry; training and download scripts are excluded.

## Usage notes

Runner parity is pending. Prefer VideoCrafter2 T2V for better visual quality from the same family, or the DiT entries for current-generation quality.
