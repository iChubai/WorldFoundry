VideoCrafter2 T2V is the improved text-to-video model of the VideoCrafter family, trained to overcome low-quality video data by combining it with high-quality image data. It is released under Apache-2.0, the most permissive license in the family. WorldFoundry runs inference only and exposes local checkpoint, fps, frame count, and seed controls.

## What it is for

Use `videocrafter2-t2v` to turn a text prompt into a short video.

A U-Net latent video diffusion model whose training decouples motion (from video) and appearance (from images), per the upstream publication. It is published by Tencent AI Lab.

Tasks: text-to-video and video generation. Aliases: `videocrafter2_t2v`. Modalities: text in, video out.

Suggested benchmarks: [EvalCrafter](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/evalcrafter) (Companion benchmark of the VideoCrafter effort), [VBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/vbench) (Standard T2V suite).

Related pages: [VideoCrafter](/docs/guides/supported-models/videocrafter) (family) and [VideoCrafter1 T2V](/docs/guides/supported-models/videocrafter1-t2v). Score on [EvalCrafter](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/evalcrafter) and [VBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/vbench).

## Runtime contract

Inputs `prompt` (bundled samples are used when omitted); options `frames`, `fps`, `height`, `width`, `steps`, `seed`, and 2 more; artifacts `generated_video` and `videocrafter2-t2v.mp4`.

CLI task profiles: `interactive-video`. WorldFoundry binds `VideoCrafter2T2VPipeline`.

Family status: **Runner parity pending**.

This is the recommended VideoCrafter variant: better quality than V1 and a clean Apache-2.0 license.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Launch with `wf run videocrafter2-t2v --pipeline.task-profile default`.

It runs in the unified environment `worldfoundry-unified-cu128` (Python 3.11, CUDA 12.8).

Weights: `VideoCrafter/VideoCrafter2`. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh videocrafter2-t2v`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
