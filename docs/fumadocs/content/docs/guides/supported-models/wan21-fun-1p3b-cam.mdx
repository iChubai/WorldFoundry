Wan2.1-Fun V1.1 1.3B Control Camera is the 1.3B camera-control model of the VideoX-Fun family: it generates video from an image along explicit camera poses. Validated with the released checkpoint: the official pose-conditioned route generates nine coherent H.264 frames at 672x384 after four denoising steps.

## What it is for

Use `wan21-fun-1p3b-cam` when the viewpoint must follow a given camera path.

A 1.3B-parameter Wan2.1 diffusion transformer with the VideoX-Fun Control+i2v camera conditioning pathway. It is published by Alibaba PAI (VideoX-Fun).

Tasks: camera-controlled video and image-to-video. Modalities: image in, video out.

Suggested benchmarks: [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench) (Camera-pose adherence for controlled i2v generation).

Related pages: [Wan2.1](/docs/guides/supported-models/wan2.1) and the larger [Wan2.1 Fun 14B Cam](/docs/guides/supported-models/wan21-fun-14b-cam). Score on [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench).

## Runtime contract

Inputs `prompt` and `input-path` (bundled samples are used when omitted); options `frames`, `fps`, `height`, `width`, `steps`, `seed`, and 1 more; artifacts `wan21-fun-1p3b-cam.mp4`.

CLI task profiles: `default`. WorldFoundry binds `Wan21Fun1P3BCameraPipeline`.

Family status: **Verified**.

This is the recommended starting point for VideoX-Fun camera control: smallest footprint and the only VideoX-Fun camera variant validated on GPU. Supply explicit camera pose sequences; scale up to the 14B/Wan2.2 variants once your pipeline works here.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Generate a camera-controlled clip with `wf run wan21-fun-1p3b-cam --pipeline.task-profile default`.

It runs in the unified environment `worldfoundry-unified-cu128` (Python 3.11, CUDA 12.8).

Weights: `alibaba-pai/Wan2.1-Fun-V1.1-1.3B-Control-Camera`. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh wan21-fun-1p3b-cam`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
