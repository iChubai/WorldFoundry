YUME is an interactive world-video generator: from an image (and for YUME-1.5, text) it produces explorable first-person world videos that respond to keyboard-style motion input. Both recorded variants are verified in this catalog — YUME I2V on the official Yume-I2V-540P checkpoint and YUME-1.5 on Yume-5B-720P — running through in-tree vendored runtime adapters under Apache-2.0.

## Architecture

Two checkpoint lines are recorded: Yume-I2V-540P for image-conditioned world video and the 5B-parameter Yume-5B-720P for the 1.5 generation with image/video/text conditioning at 720P. Architecture internals beyond the vendored adapters are not recorded in this repository.

## Usage notes

Both variants share the pipeline runner and unified environment profile; checkpoints stage into WORLDFOUNDRY_CKPT_DIR. Prefer yume-1p5 for text conditioning and 720P output. Per-run visual QA is tracked outside the catalog.
