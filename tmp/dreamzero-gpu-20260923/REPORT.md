# DreamZero DROID and AgiBot real-GPU validation

The public full DreamZero-DROID and DreamZero-AgiBot checkpoints each loaded all ten model shards and completed one WorldFoundry `GrootSimPolicy.lazy_joint_forward_causal` pass on separate H100 GPUs (1 and 2). The two runs finished in about 475 seconds each, including about 405 seconds of checkpoint loading. They completed eight DiT denoising steps, produced finite action chunks and future-video latents, and decoded and re-read nine 640×352 RGB frames each at 5 fps. See the independent [DROID result](droid/result.json) and [AgiBot result](agibot/result.json), plus their [status files](droid/status.json) and [status files](agibot/status.json).

The input was one real toy photograph repeated under each checkpoint's three camera keys, synthetic zero robot state, and the instruction to move the gripper toward the table object. DROID returned 24×7 joint positions and 24 gripper positions; AgiBot returned 48-step outputs for both arms, both effectors, head, waist, and robot velocity. Both checkpoints returned a finite 1×16×3×44×80 future-video latent. The generated videos are [DROID](droid/future_world.mp4) (SHA-256 `1cc4ae70dc5a939c34926bb72d89a3b4a5c993ec8681a0e5ba4fca4e5385ea99`) and [AgiBot](agibot/future_world.mp4) (SHA-256 `8d765cc337e824faabaa4a1e7b50cecf1d8d94f2fd0634db502476b070f6cc63`).

I inspected frames 0, 4, and 8 in the [DROID contact](droid/contact-0-4-8.jpg) and [AgiBot contact](agibot/contact-0-4-8.jpg). The toy, plate, red table, and wall remain recognizable in the populated camera views. DROID's upper view shifts and blurs mildly; AgiBot has a black fourth grid tile because its three views are padded into a 2×2 mosaic. The [DROID review](droid/quality-review.json) and [AgiBot review](agibot/quality-review.json) record frame counts, hashes, sampled temporal differences, and the tile check. This is **verified inference only**: repeated photographs and zero states do not measure action correctness, multi-camera fidelity, or robot-task success.

The first five rounds exposed and led to repairs in the project runtime: full-checkpoint component loading, CLIP visual compilation, VAE device arguments, DreamZero variable-length attention fallback, and the FlowUniPC scheduler call signature. The sixth round completed after these repairs. The scheduler fix also passed a separate three-step finite-output test for standard and decoupled-noise schedules. Logs are [DROID](droid-sixth.log) and [AgiBot](agibot-sixth.log); the reproducible probe is [run_case.py](run_case.py). Temporary `tianshou` and `pettingzoo` dependencies were installed under this ignored evidence directory without altering the shared environment.
