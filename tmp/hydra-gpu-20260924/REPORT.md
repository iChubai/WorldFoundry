# HyDRA GPU validation

The public `HydraPipeline` loaded the local `H-EmbodVis/HyDRA` checkpoint and Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B DiT, text encoder, and VAE through the bundled runtime on physical GPU 3. The HyDRA checkpoint is loaded with `strict=True`. The conditioning video was the previously generated 81-frame LongSANA room clip; its first 77 frames were paired with a synthetic 20-pose camera JSON containing a small rightward and forward translation. This is an out-of-domain, synthetic-control fixture, not an official HyDRA example or camera benchmark.

The first 4-step smoke run generated [demo-smoke.mp4](demo-smoke.mp4), a 154-frame output at 832×480 and 15 FPS. Frames 0–76 are the condition video; frames 77–153 are the generated continuation. The [smoke contact sheet](contact-smoke.jpg) revealed a large dark wall-like occlusion in the generated middle frames, so the run was repeated at the upstream default of 50 denoising steps. The full [demo-quality.mp4](demo-quality.mp4) completed 50/50 steps and all 154 frames decode. [validation-quality.json](validation-quality.json) records the frame split and numeric checks; [demo-quality-result.json](demo-quality-result.json) records the public pipeline call. In the [quality contact sheet](contact-quality.jpg), the room is recognizable at the beginning and end of the generated half, but a broad dark wall fills most of generated frame 96 and nearby middle frames. The artifact therefore has a visible quality concern even at 50 steps.

This validates local checkpoint-backed loading, camera input plumbing, denoising, VAE decoding, and MP4 export for this one configuration. The generated half alone was reviewed; the clear conditioning half was not used as evidence of generation quality. The synthetic camera path and model-generated source clip do not establish camera adherence, official-data quality, or general trajectory behavior. The pinned upstream source has no root LICENSE file, so its license remains unresolved.
