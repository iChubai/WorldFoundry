# Solaris single-GPU validation

The in-tree Solaris inference runtime loaded the public `nyu-visionx/solaris` Orbax checkpoints (`clip.pt`, `vae.pt`, `solaris.pt`) and one example from the official `solaris-eval-datasets` translation set. JAX 0.4.35, Flax 0.10.2, and Orbax 0.11.5 in `../envs/worldarena-trajan` saw one H100 through `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`.

The translation run produced [a 33-frame, 1280×704, 20 FPS comparison video](output/single_gpu_translation_33f/eval_translation/video_0_side_by_side.mp4) in 354 seconds including first compilation. The left half is ground truth and the right half is model output; the upper and lower panels show the two players. Viewed [frame 0](translation-frame-000.png), [frame 16](translation-frame-016.png), and [frame 32](translation-frame-032.png). Both generated views retain the flat Minecraft terrain, sky, HUD, and visible avatars while the avatars move; there is no obvious scene collapse in this short clip. See [translation status](translation-status.json) and [runner result](result.json).

An unconditional, unused `wandb` import in `base_runner.py` prevented this inference-only runtime from starting in the available JAX environment. Removing it allowed the full runner import and real checkpoint restore. The test used one official sample, 33 evaluation frames, no data workers, and the runtime's default four denoising steps. The JAX allocator emitted a nonfatal 37 GB cuDNN algorithm-search allocation warning; inference completed and the output video decoded.

The [rotationEval comparison](output/single_gpu_rotation_33f/eval_rotation/video_0_side_by_side.mp4) also decoded as 33 frames at 1280×704/20 FPS (325 seconds including a fresh JAX compilation). Viewed [frame 0](rotation-frame-000.png), [frame 16](rotation-frame-016.png), and [frame 32](rotation-frame-032.png). The upper generated view keeps the Minecraft scene, but the other player's avatar shifts to the far right edge by frame 16 while the ground truth avatar stays nearer the center. The mismatch persists at frame 32. The upper GT-versus-output mean absolute pixel difference rises from 2.99 at frame 0 to 7.35 at frame 16; the [frame-difference audit](rotation-frame-diff.json) confirms output motion rather than a fully frozen video. I record this one case as a quality concern, not a quantitative action score. See [rotation status](rotation-status.json) and [runner result](result-rotation.json).

The [oneLooksAwayEval comparison](output/single_gpu_one_looks_away_33f/eval_one_looks_away/video_0_side_by_side.mp4) decoded as another 33-frame, 1280×704/20 FPS clip (329 seconds). Viewed [frame 0](one-looks-away-frame-000.png), [frame 16](one-looks-away-frame-016.png), and [frame 32](one-looks-away-frame-032.png). The flat world and lower player's view remain stable, but the upper generated view loses the visible other avatar by frame 16 while ground truth still shows it near the right edge. I record this one sample as a camera/action alignment quality concern; see [status](one-looks-away-status.json) and [runner result](result-one_looks_away.json).

These short qualitative samples do not measure action-control accuracy. Four additional official categories completed together in 773 seconds. Each produced a decodable 33-frame, 1280×704, 20 FPS side-by-side comparison. Frames 0, 16 and 32 were visually reviewed for each category. [structure](output/single_gpu_remaining4_33f/eval_structure/video_0_side_by_side.mp4) retains the world but the other player drifts relative to GT at frame 16. [turn_to_look](output/single_gpu_remaining4_33f/eval_turn_to_look/video_0_side_by_side.mp4) and [turn_to_look_opposite](output/single_gpu_remaining4_33f/eval_turn_to_look_opposite/video_0_side_by_side.mp4) retain recognizable Minecraft scenes, yet both generated camera/terrain views diverge substantially from GT by frames 16 and 32. These three are recorded as quality concerns. [both_look_away](output/single_gpu_remaining4_33f/eval_both_look_away/video_0_side_by_side.mp4) retains stable flat terrain, sky and HUD while the avatars leave view in both GT and generated panels; it is recorded as verified inference only. See [contact sheet](remaining4-contact.png), per-category status JSON files, and [runner result](result-remaining4.json). These four single clips do not establish benchmark scores or broad action-control accuracy.
